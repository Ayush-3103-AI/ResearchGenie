## Known limitations
- This report is constrained by the small corpus size (8 papers) and the fact that many relevant studies found in the verification search (e.g., [9]-[20]) were not part of the original retrieval for the 'blue light' query but were retrieved specifically to test author-flagged candidates. Consequently, some gaps identified may reflect retrieval bias rather than true absence from the literature. Additionally, the corpus contains a mix of preprints and peer-reviewed articles with varying methodological rigor.
- 2 reference(s) had no DOI, or got no answer from the lookup providers, and could not be automatically verified against Crossref/OpenAlex.
- 2 reference(s) could not be matched back to a Discovery paper record by DOI, so claims citing them could not be checked against original evidence.
